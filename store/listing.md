## Full description (max 4000)
Color Life turns a classic cellular automaton into a puzzle you can solve in a minute.

Every square on the board is a cell. Each step, all cells change at once by four simple rules: lonely cells die, crowded cells die, cells with two or three neighbours survive, and an empty square with exactly three neighbours comes to life. Your job is to predict what happens next. Mark the cells that will die and the squares where new cells are born, then press Check and watch your prediction play out.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a live cell to mark it as dying, tap an empty square to mark a birth
• Live cells you leave unmarked count as survivors
• Check your answer: each wrong guess costs a heart and shows you which cells were wrong
• Solve with all three hearts for a perfect score

MODES
• Play: six difficulties, from a 5×5 warm-up to 10×10 boards with 26 blocks
• Daily puzzle: one new puzzle a day for everyone, with a streak to keep alive
• Endless: your answer becomes the next puzzle. How long can your pattern last?
• Rush: solve as many as you can in 60 seconds
• Playground: draw your own patterns and watch them evolve

ALSO INSIDE
• A short tutorial that teaches the rules step by step
• Abilities when you're stuck: Peek, Counter, Paintball, Rocket, Birth scan and Shield
• Replays at 0.5× to 3× speed, and a review that explains every mistake
• Challenge a friend with a link to the exact puzzle you just solved
• Light, dark and unlockable colour themes
• Pinch to zoom on big boards
• No account needed. Progress is saved on your phone.

Coins come from solving puzzles, the daily bonus, and optional rewarded ads. Coin packs are available as an optional purchase.
