Themes

Earn coins with every star, then unlock new colours.

How to play

Each cell looks at its 8 neighbours (diagonals too). Predict what every cell does in the next step.

Lonely: dies

A live cell with 0 or 1 live neighbours dies.

2 or 3: survives

A live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive.

Crowded: dies

A live cell with 4 or more live neighbours dies.

Exactly 3: born

An empty cell with exactly 3 live neighbours comes to life.

How to answer

  • ●Tap a white cell once: it survives
  • ✕Tap it twice: it dies
  • +Tap an empty cell: it's born

Modes

  • Play Pick a difficulty from Very easy to Extreme: harder means a bigger board with more blocks. Too many mistakes cost a heart. After each puzzle, watch the board zoom out and play on.
  • Endless Your answer becomes the next puzzle, so you follow one pattern step after step. You have 3 lives per run. Here the board's edge is a wall: nothing exists outside it.
  • Daily Everyone gets the same puzzle today. Share your result and keep your streak.
  • Rush Solve as many as you can in 60 seconds. Only perfect answers count.
  • Playground Free play, no scores. Draw any pattern on an endless board and press Start to watch the rules play out. Use Next and Undo to go one step at a time, and turn on Colour hints to see what survives, dies and is born next. A good place to practise, test a guess, or just watch famous patterns like the glider walk across the board.
