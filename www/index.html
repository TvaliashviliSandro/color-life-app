How to play

Each cell looks at its 8 neighbours (diagonals too). Predict what every cell does in the next step.

Lonely: dies

A live cell with 0 or 1 live neighbours dies.

2 or 3: survives

A live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive. Leave it unmarked.

Crowded: dies

A live cell with 4 or more live neighbours dies.

Exactly 3: born

An empty cell with exactly 3 live neighbours comes to life.

How to answer

  • ✕Tap a live cell: it dies (tap again to undo)
  • +Tap an empty cell: it's born
  • Leave everything else: it survives or stays empty
  • Big board? Pinch to zoom in, drag to move, pinch out to see it all

Abilities

    Modes

    • Play Pick a difficulty from Very easy to Extreme: harder means a bigger board with more blocks. Each puzzle gives you 3 hearts; every Check with mistakes costs one, and the wrong cells get flagged so you can fix them. Your stars are the hearts you have left.
    • Endless Your answer becomes the next puzzle, so you follow one pattern step after step. You have 3 hearts for the whole run. Here the board's edge is a wall: nothing exists outside it.
    • Daily Everyone gets the same puzzle today. Share your result and keep your streak.
    • Rush Solve as many as you can in 60 seconds. Only perfect answers count, and abilities are off.
    • Playground Free play, no scores. Draw any pattern on an endless board and press Start to watch the rules play out. Use Next and Undo to go one step at a time, and turn on Colour hints to see what dies and what is born next. A good place to practise, test a guess, or just watch famous patterns like the glider walk across the board.
