The 4 main principles

Every cell looks at its 8 neighbours (the squares touching it, diagonals included). All cells update at the same time, once per generation.

1. Loneliness

A live cell with 0 or 1 live neighbours dies — it has too few friends.

2. Survival

A live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive into the next generation.

3. Overcrowding

A live cell with 4 or more live neighbours dies — it is too crowded.

4. Birth

An empty cell with exactly 3 live neighbours comes to life.

In the game the colours show what happens next: stays alive, dies, is born.
