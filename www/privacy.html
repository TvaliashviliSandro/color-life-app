Privacy Policy

Color Life: Predict the Step · Last updated 26 November 2026

Color Life is a puzzle game. We don't ask for an account, your name, email address, contacts, location, or any other personal information. This page explains the little data that is involved.

1. Your game progress stays on your device

Coins, stars, streaks, settings and unlocked themes are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. We have no server that receives them. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes them.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android app can show optional rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example to get a heart back). These are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about the ad interaction, and may use it for personalised ads where the law allows. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first through Google's consent form.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs are sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the coins can be added.

4. Sharing a puzzle

When you tap "Challenge a friend" or share a daily result, the app creates a link or text that describes the puzzle board (and your heart score). It only leaves your phone if you choose to send it, through the app you pick.

5. Children

The game is suitable for everyone. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with a "General audiences" content rating.

6. Web version

The version that runs in a web browser has no real ads or purchases; anything shown there is a labelled test. It saves progress in your browser's local storage only.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/TvaliashviliSandro/color-life-app/issues.
